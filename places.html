Top Places in Lahore

6 Famous Historical Places You Must Visit

Badshahi Mosque

Built in 1673, one of the largest mosques in Pakistan.

Shahi Qila

Historic fort of Mughal Empire, UNESCO World Heritage.

Food Street

Famous food street near Badshahi Mosque.

Minar-e-Pakistan

Iconic tower in Greater Iqbal Park, Lahore.

Wazir Khan Mosque

Beautiful mosque famous for its tile work.

Lahore Museum

Largest museum in Pakistan, full of history.
